STEM RACING BRAND GUIDELINES INTRODUCTION OVERVIEW Designed by honcho.agency Overview Were excited to share our updated brand identity which marks a new chapter for our organisation under a new name, STEM Racing. Our vision for this rebrand is to shift gears, taking us beyond where weve been. Our new identity will act as a window into our unique personality and purpose, helping the community we serve understand us better. In this new venture, its hugely important that anyone responsible for communicating our brand follows these guidelines, as only through consistent application will it become recognised, understood and loved. Lets get started. If youre unsure about anything in these guidelines or need help with applying them, please get in touch with our marketing team.

STEM RACING BRAND GUIDELINES 02. OUR LOGOS LOGO RESTRICTIONS Logo restrictions The exclusion zone is an invisible area surrounding our logo, in which no other text, imagery or graphics can appear. Its calculated using the unit (x), which is half the height of the logo as shown. Always observe this clear space as a minimum and where possible, increase it. Although our logo doesnt have a maximum size, it shouldnt appear any smaller than 10mm or 24px in height or 70px in width. EXCLUSION ZONE 10MM / 24PX MINIMUM 11

Logo misuse This is a guide of common mistakes to avoid when using the logo. Avoiding these misuses will ensure our logo is used consistently, maintaining its integrity. Do not put a stroke on the logo. Do not ignore the exclusion zone. Do not use non-approved colours. Do not stretch the logo horizontally or vertically. Do not rotate the wordmark or icon. Motion effect Our image style plays a leading role in presenting STEM Racing as a fast-paced and dynamic organisation. We use this effect to create a sense of speed, motion and to provide a clear focal point on our image subjects. This effect can be applied to all styles of photography, both full image and cut-out. Applying the motion effect This motion effect is created through the use of a path blur effect, which is erased around the focal point of the image and blended with the original photo. The effect is achieved in four steps. Duplicate the original image. 2. In Photoshop, apply a path blur. Adjust the parameters to the desired angle and speed. The angle should be dictated by the composition of the image, following the natural shapes to highlight the subject. Speed controls the length of the blur, higher values give the impression of higher speeds. 3. Apply a layer mask to the blurred image, and erase to reveal the focal point of the original image. 4. Experiment with the opacity and blend mode of the blurred layer so the effect is prominent, but not overpowering.
